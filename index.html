BSI Risk Simulator
E. coli and Enterococcus bloodstream-infection forecasts from stool samples · allo-HCT, MSK · research prototype, not for clinical use

What the selected alert threshold would have meant across the whole retrospective cohort (every stool sample scored by a model that never saw that patient): how many alerts, how often an alert was followed by an infection, and how many infected patients were warned in advance.

How to use this simulator

  1. Pick a case. Cases are real, de-identified patients from the MSK allo-HCT cohort. Outcomes are hidden.
  2. Step through the stay with the ◀ / ▶ arrows (or the ← / → keys, or the slider). Each new stool sample updates two risk scores: the probability of an E. coli and of an Enterococcus bloodstream infection in the next 14 days. You only see data up to the current day: microbiome composition, antibiotics, neutrophil counts and temperature.
  3. When a score crosses the alert threshold, the panel on the right shows what drove it and asks what you would do. Your answers stay in this browser; you can download them.
  4. Reveal the outcome at any point, or keep stepping: once the replay passes an E. coli or Enterococcus infection, the outcome is revealed automatically.
  5. The Ward view shows the alert burden and catch rate of the chosen threshold across all patients.

Retrospective research prototype. Scores were produced by models that never saw the patient being shown (cross-validation), but the alert thresholds were chosen on this same cohort, and the models have not been validated prospectively or at another center.
